FRAGCUT - QUICK GUIDE
=====================
Cut the best moments out of your game recordings and stitch them into one video.
Right-click a recording, watch, press I / O / C. Free, no account, nothing runs in the background.


INSTALL
-------
1. Unzip the whole folder (right-click the zip > Extract All).
2. Double-click Install.bat.
   - "Windows protected your PC"?  Click "More info" > "Run anyway" (FragCut is not code-signed).
   - No ffmpeg (the free video engine FragCut uses)?  The installer offers to install it. Say Y.
3. Done. You get:
   - right-click a video > Send to > FragCut / FragCut - Join clips / FragCut - Cut by time / FragCut - Compress
   - "FragCut" on the Desktop
   Windows 11: "Send to" is under "Show more options" (or hold Shift while right-clicking).


FRAGCUT - watch, mark, cut
--------------------------
Select one or more recordings > right-click > Send to > FragCut.
The video plays. The flow:  I (start) -> O (end) -> the marked part loops -> fine-tune -> Enter.
Slices save in the background (next to the recording) while you keep watching.

  Space ............ play / pause
  Left / Right ..... back / forward 0.5 s   (Shift = 5 s, Ctrl = 30 s)
  , / . ............ one frame back / forward
  I ................ mark start
  O ................ mark end  -> the marked part loops
  Q / W ............ start earlier / later   (0.2 s, Shift = 1 s)
  E / R ............ end earlier / later     (0.2 s, Shift = 1 s)
  Enter or C ....... cut the slice and keep watching   (only a start marked = cut up to now)
  Esc .............. clear the marks
  P / N ............ previous / next recording
  J ................ join the slices you cut into one video
  Up / Down ........ playback speed: 0.25x, 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 4x
  + / - ............ volume

  Click anywhere on the timeline to jump there, or drag it (pauses + mutes while dragging).
  Opened several recordings? They're listed on the right - click one to switch.
  "Also save MP3" = also save the sound of each slice as an MP3.
  The orange bar above the timeline shows the marked part.
  Slices are named like:  <recording> - clip 01m38-01m43.mp4


JOIN CLIPS - stitch slices together
-----------------------------------
Press J in FragCut, or select slices in Explorer > right-click > Send to > FragCut - Join clips.
- Lists the slices in recording order. Press Enter to keep it, or type a new order: 3 1 2
  (leave a number out to skip that slice).
- Slices made by FragCut join instantly with no quality loss.
- Slices with different resolution / fps are re-encoded automatically.
- Saved as:  <first recording> - montage.mp4   (optionally + MP3)


CUT BY TIME - type the times instead
------------------------------------
Right-click a video > Send to > FragCut - Cut by time, then type start and end:
98   1:38   1:01:38   1:38.5   (empty end = until the end). Saves an MP4 + MP3.


COMPRESS - smaller file to share
--------------------------------
Select one or more videos > right-click > Send to > FragCut - Compress.
- Makes a copy about half the size that looks nearly the same (H.264, plays everywhere).
- Saved as:  <name> - compressed.mp4   next to the original. The original is not changed.
- Game recordings and montages shrink the most. Already-small videos are skipped.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Encoding uses your graphics card when it can (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel), otherwise the CPU (slower, same result).
  The window title shows which one is used.
- Video black / won't play in FragCut?  It's probably HEVC/H.265 or AV1: install "HEVC Video Extensions"
  or "AV1 Video Extension" from the Microsoft Store, or record in H.264.
- OBS open?  Slices are cut gently so OBS recordings don't stutter (they take a moment longer to save).
- Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Everything runs on your PC.
- Manual ffmpeg install: open PowerShell and run   winget install Gyan.FFmpeg.Essentials   then reopen FragCut.


UNINSTALL
---------
Run Uninstall.bat (in the unzipped folder, or in %LOCALAPPDATA%\FragCut).
Removes the shortcuts and FragCut. Your clips are not touched.
