FRAGCUT - QUICK GUIDE
=====================
Cut the best moments out of your game recordings and stitch them into one video.
Right-click a recording, watch, press I / O / C. Free, no account, nothing runs in the background.


INSTALL
-------
1. Unzip the whole folder (right-click the zip > Extract All).
2. Double-click Install.bat.
   - "Windows protected your PC"?  Click "More info" > "Run anyway" (FragCut is not code-signed).
   - A window opens. It shows the two free programs FragCut uses and whether they're installed:
     ffmpeg (required: cuts, joins, compresses) and mpv (recommended: plays the recordings, including AV1/HEVC).
     Leave the missing ones ticked and click Install. ffmpeg comes from winget; mpv is downloaded from its
     official Windows builds (github.com/shinchiro/mpv-winbuild-cmake) into FragCut's folder.
3. Done. You get:
   - right-click a video > Send to > FragCut / FragCut - Join clips / FragCut - Cut by time / FragCut - Compress
     / FragCut - Kill montage
   - "FragCut" on the Desktop
   Windows 11: "Send to" is under "Show more options" (or hold Shift while right-clicking).


FRAGCUT - watch, mark, cut
--------------------------
Select one or more recordings > right-click > Send to > FragCut.
The video plays. The flow:  I (start) -> O (end) -> the marked part loops -> fine-tune -> Enter.
Slices save in the background (next to the recording) while you keep watching.

  Space ............ play / pause
  Left / Right ..... back / forward 0.5 s   (Shift = 5 s, Ctrl = 30 s)
  , / . ............ one frame back / forward
  I ................ mark start
  O ................ mark end  -> the marked part loops
  Q / W ............ start earlier / later   (0.2 s, Shift = 1 s)
  E / R ............ end earlier / later     (0.2 s, Shift = 1 s)
  Enter or C ....... cut the slice and keep watching   (only a start marked = cut up to now)
  Esc .............. clear the marks
  P / N ............ previous / next recording
  J ................ join the slices you cut into one video
  K ................ find kills (Battlefield 6), then jump to the next kill   (Shift+K = previous)
  Up / Down ........ playback speed: 0.25x, 0.5x, 1x, 2x, 4x
  + / - ............ volume

  Click anywhere on the timeline to jump there, or drag it (pauses + mutes while dragging).
  Opened several recordings? They're listed on the right - click one to switch.
  "Also save MP3" = also save the sound of each slice as an MP3.
  "Fast cut (no re-encode)" = copy the slice out of the recording instead of encoding it: saved instantly,
     original quality and size. It can only start on a keyframe, so the start moves back to the last one
     before your mark (usually 0.5-2 s), and Q / W move the start by one keyframe. The clip keeps the
     recording's format (AV1 / HEVC clips don't play everywhere).
  The orange bar above the timeline shows the marked part. Drag its left / right end to move the start / end.
  Slices are saved in a "clips" folder next to the recording, named like:  <recording> - clip 01m38-01m43.mp4


FOLDERS AND ROUNDS
------------------
"Open folder..." lists all recordings in a folder, oldest first (or right-click a folder > Send to > FragCut).
To keep one game round together: select its recordings in the list (Ctrl+click / Shift+click) > "Make round...".
Type a short game tag (BF6, APEX ...); FragCut suggests the next round number and shows the new names first.
  BF6-12 2026-11-13\                       the round's folder (tag, round number, date)
    BF6-12-1 <original name>.mp4           recordings, numbered in the order they were recorded
    clips\BF6-12-1 clip 01m38-01m43.mp4    clips cut from them (also the ones cut before)
Joining a round's clips gives "BF6-12 montage.mp4". "Undo round" moves the last round's files back.


FIND KILLS - Battlefield 6
-------------------------
Press K. FragCut looks for the KILL / HEADSHOT / KILL CONFIRMED tags and puts a red mark on the
timeline for each kill (you can keep watching; a 2-minute recording takes about 10 seconds).
Then K jumps to the next kill and marks 8 s before it to 2 s after, on repeat. Enter saves it.
Shift+K goes back. Results are remembered. Needs a 16:9 recording and the default HUD.

KILL MONTAGE: select recordings > right-click > Send to > FragCut - Kill montage.
Finds the kills, takes 5 s before to 1 s after each, and joins them into
<first recording> - kill montage.mp4. No questions asked.


JOIN CLIPS - stitch slices together
-----------------------------------
Press J in FragCut, or select slices in Explorer > right-click > Send to > FragCut - Join clips.
- Lists the slices in recording order. Press Enter to keep it, or type a new order: 3 1 2
  (leave a number out to skip that slice).
- Slices made by FragCut join instantly with no quality loss.
- Slices with different resolution / fps are re-encoded automatically.
- Saved as:  <first recording> - montage.mp4   (optionally + MP3)


CUT BY TIME - type the times instead
------------------------------------
Right-click a video > Send to > FragCut - Cut by time, then type start and end:
98   1:38   1:01:38   1:38.5   (empty end = until the end). Saves an MP4 + MP3.


COMPRESS - smaller file to share
--------------------------------
Select one or more videos > right-click > Send to > FragCut - Compress.
- Makes a copy about half the size that looks nearly the same (H.264, plays everywhere).
- Saved as:  <name> - compressed.mp4   next to the original. The original is not changed.
- Game recordings and montages shrink the most. Already-small videos are skipped, and so are AV1/HEVC/VP9
  videos (already more compact than the H.264 copy).


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Encoding uses your graphics card when it can (NVIDIA, AMD or Intel), otherwise the CPU (slower, same result).
  The window title shows which one is used.
- The player is mpv if it's installed (the window title says "player: mpv"); it plays H.264, HEVC, AV1 and VP9.
  Without mpv, FragCut uses Windows' player. That one can't play some recordings (often AV1 or HEVC/H.265),
  so FragCut makes an H.264 "preview copy" to play, once per recording. Clips are still cut from the original.
  Install mpv to skip this: run Install.bat again.
- OBS open?  Slices are cut gently so OBS recordings don't stutter (they take a moment longer to save).
- Nothing is uploaded anywhere. Everything runs on your PC.
- Manual ffmpeg install: open PowerShell and run   winget install Gyan.FFmpeg.Essentials   then reopen FragCut.


UNINSTALL
---------
Run Uninstall.bat (in the unzipped folder, or in %LOCALAPPDATA%\FragCut).
Removes the shortcuts and FragCut, including its copy of mpv. Your clips are not touched, and ffmpeg stays installed.
